==================================================
   Wallpaper Sync for Mac - Quick Start Guide
==================================================

1. Double-click "Install_Mac.command"
2. When Terminal opens, press [Enter]
3. Done! Wallpaper Sync is now running in the background.

Whenever you change a wallpaper on your phone using the
Wallpaper Sync Android App, your Mac will automatically
sync and update within 3 seconds!

To uninstall anytime, simply double-click "Uninstall_Mac.command".
